What AURA provides

Introducing AURA — Your One-Stop Shop for Data Access at WBD

AURA (Automated Unified Resource Access) is WBD's centralized platform for requesting and managing access to data products across our key platforms — including Databricks, Snowflake, Looker, and Tableau.

Whether you need to spin up engineering pipelines, explore datasets, or simply view dashboards, AURA streamlines the entire process from request to approval.

With AURA, users can submit access requests with the appropriate role (Data/ML Engineer, Analytics Engineer, Dashboard Explorer, and more), track request status in real time, and even handle approvals — all from a single interface. The platform also supports multi-level approval workflows involving Line Managers, Data Product Approvers, and Data Governance Approvers to ensure proper oversight of sensitive data.

What's updated

🔒 Stricter Access Controls for Data Product Approver Management

Only approvers who are directly assigned to a data product can now manage its approver list — and they can only manage approvers at their own privilege level or below.

  • Data Product Owners can add or remove Primary and Secondary Approvers
  • Primary Approvers can add or remove Secondary Approvers only
  • Secondary Approvers cannot make changes to the approver list

📬 Rejection Emails Now Always Delivered

When a Line Manager or Data Product Owner declined a request, the rejection email to the requester was sometimes not sent. This has been fixed — the requester will now always receive a rejection notification.

📋 Approval Queue No Longer Shows Stale Items

After one Data Product Owner approved an access request, the same request sometimes remained visible in the queues of other product owners for that product. Once the request is actioned, all queues are now updated immediately.

🔍 Track Requests Page Now Shows All Pending Requests

Requests that were in intermediate approval stages (e.g. awaiting Data Product Owner, awaiting Business Approver) were not always visible on the Track Requests page. All pending requests are now correctly displayed.

🔄 New Approvers Immediately Receive Pending Requests

When a new approver was added to a data product, any access requests already in progress were not showing up in their approval queue. New approvers will now see all relevant pending requests straight away.

Tip of the Month

💡 Tip of the Month: Does Your Entire Product Team Need Access?

If your whole product team needs access to AURA data products—such as Privacy—there's a simpler and more scalable approach than submitting individual access requests.

Instead of requesting multiple ML/Data Engineer roles (which should be the exception, not the rule), use Cross Catalog Data Product Access. If you already have an ML/Data Engineer role from onboarding, you can self-service access to additional data products by associating that role with the appropriate catalog through GitHub.

Why this works better:

  • ✅ Team-wide access — Permissions are granted at the functional group level, so your entire team inherits access.
  • ✅ Cleaner role management — Avoids role sprawl by reusing existing ML/Data Engineer roles whenever possible.
  • ✅ Simpler onboarding — New team members automatically receive access when added to the group.
  • ✅ Lower operational overhead — Fewer approvals and no repeated individual requests.

📘 Learn how to get started: Self-Service Databricks Cross Catalog Access

Note: GitHub pull requests still require approval from the data product owner before access is granted.
Common questions

How do I access AURA?

Go to https://backstage.prd.o11y.discomax.com/aura in Google Chrome while connected to the corporate VPN. Other browsers or off-VPN connections may not load the app.

Why can't I open the AURA page?

Confirm your corporate VPN is connected, switch to Chrome, and try the URL again. If it still fails, contact your IT or data platform support team.

How do I track my access request?

To track your request in AURA, go to Access Requests → Track Request History, and click the Details button next to your request to see real-time status and approval updates

How do I report a bug or request a feature?

Use the feedback option in AURA or email aura-support@wbd.com with the request ID and a short description of the issue.
