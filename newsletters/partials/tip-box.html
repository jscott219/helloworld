💡 Tip of the Month: Does Your Entire Product Team Need Access?

If your whole product team needs access to AURA data products—such as Privacy—there's a simpler and more scalable approach than submitting individual access requests.

Instead of requesting multiple ML/Data Engineer roles (which should be the exception, not the rule), use Cross Catalog Data Product Access. If you already have an ML/Data Engineer role from onboarding, you can self-service access to additional data products by associating that role with the appropriate catalog through GitHub.

Why this works better:

  • ✅ Team-wide access — Permissions are granted at the functional group level, so your entire team inherits access.
  • ✅ Cleaner role management — Avoids role sprawl by reusing existing ML/Data Engineer roles whenever possible.
  • ✅ Simpler onboarding — New team members automatically receive access when added to the group.
  • ✅ Lower operational overhead — Fewer approvals and no repeated individual requests.

📘 Learn how to get started: Self-Service Databricks Cross Catalog Access

Note: GitHub pull requests still require approval from the data product owner before access is granted.
